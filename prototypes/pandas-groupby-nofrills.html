Lesson

1. The problem

Your PM asks: "What's our total weight in each region?" You have one row per stock:

tickerregionsectorweight
AAAEuropeTech5%
BBBAsiaBanks3%
CCCEuropeHealth4%
DDDEMTech2%
EEEAsiaHealth5%
FFFEMBanks1%

Adding by hand works for 6 rows, not for 600. pandas does it in one line:

df.groupby('region')['weight'].sum()

2. What that line does

  1. Split. groupby('region') sorts the rows into piles, one pile per region. Nothing is added up yet.
  2. Apply. ['weight'].sum() adds up the weight inside each pile.
  3. Combine. The totals come back as one small table, one row per region.
regionrows in the pilesum
EuropeAAA 5, CCC 49
AsiaBBB 3, EEE 58
EMDDD 2, FFF 13

3. Swap the last word

The piles stay the same. Only the math done on each pile changes.

region.sum().mean().count().max()
Europe94.525
Asia84.025
EM31.522

Questions

1. What does this give you? 
df.groupby('region')['weight'].mean()

2. In what order do these happen? 
df.groupby('region')['weight'].sum()
